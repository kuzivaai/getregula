# Document and repository permission draft

STATUS: PREPARATION ONLY
EXTERNAL ACTION: DISABLED
VENTURE DECISION: STOP
PRODUCT PILOT: NOT APPROVED

LEGAL REVIEW: REQUIRED. REAL DATA COLLECTION: DISABLED.

Record owner/authorised discloser, exact artefact and hash, purpose, permitted
users/actions, excluded paths/data, confidentiality, third-party restrictions,
secret-scan process, storage, expiry, derived-label rights, publication/reuse
choices and return/deletion. Access is read-only by default. Permission does not
prove ownership and does not authorise legal classification or production use.
