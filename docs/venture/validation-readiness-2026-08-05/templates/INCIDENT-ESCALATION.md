# Incident escalation draft

STATUS: PREPARATION ONLY

LEGAL REVIEW: REQUIRED. DATA-PROTECTION REVIEW: REQUIRED. REAL DATA COLLECTION:
DISABLED.

Stop access; protect people; preserve minimal evidence; revoke credentials where
safe; record time, systems, categories, volume, recipients and containment;
notify the authorised owner and adviser; assess contractual, data-protection and
security duties; document decision and remediation. This template does not decide
whether regulator, participant, insurer or police notification is required.
