## Concepts imported and bounded

| Concept | Mechanism | Applicability | Limitation and alternative | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Most-recent-event interviewing | Anchors answers in an observable transaction | Tests the exact onboarding hypothesis | Recall remains imperfect; documents are stronger where permissioned | Ask for event evidence, not market opinions |
| Assurance-case structure | Separates claim, evidence, challenge and decision | Fits evidence-readiness work | Does not make assertions true; a flat question/evidence table is simpler | Use a flat traceability unit initially |
| Blinded paired comparison | Reduces label contamination and compares same units | Suitable after a frozen tool and manual baseline exist | Carryover and clustering complicate inference | Manual labels are frozen before outputs open |
| Prospective holdout | Reduces benchmark leakage | Necessary because existing fixtures shaped development | Access may constrain representativeness | Freeze version and corpus manifest before labels open |
