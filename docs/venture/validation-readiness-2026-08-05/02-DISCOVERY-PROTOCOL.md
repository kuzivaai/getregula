> A small UK AI supplier is responding to a UK general insurer’s production-onboarding evidence request for an AI-assisted claims-triage system.

This is `PREREGISTERED_NOT_EXECUTED`. It is not a validated segment.

## Roles and transaction

- Supplier participant: directly performed or owned the most recent evidence response.
- Buyer participant: directly requested, reviewed or decided on that evidence for a general insurer.
- Blocker: person or function able to pause or reject onboarding.
- Approver: person or committee with documented decision authority.
- Trigger: a specific production-onboarding review, not general interest or a hypothetical purchase.

Include only UK transactions within the preceding 18 months involving a small
supplier, a general insurer, production onboarding and AI-assisted claims triage.
For screening, “small” means fewer than 50 employees at the transaction date;
this operational boundary is a research choice, not a claim that the market uses
that definition. The 18-month window trades recall for fresher documentary and
participant memory and must be reported as a limitation.
Exclude banks, brokers, life-only insurers, generic AI policy discussions,
pre-product ideation, recruitment AI, fraud-only systems, and participants without
first-hand involvement. A disqualifier is absence of a real transaction, no
permission to discuss it safely, or a standard short form requiring no material
evidence work.

## Evidence tests

| Proposition | Minimum future evidence | Contrary evidence | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transaction occurred | Redacted request, workflow record or corroborated first-hand account with date and roles | Opinion without an event | Exclude from transaction analysis |
| Delay or rejection occurred | Dated challenge, rework, decision or elapsed-time record | Onboarding proceeded without material evidence work | Counts against pain hypothesis |
| Economic impact occurred | Recorded staff time, delayed revenue/cost or paid workaround with basis | Guessed monetary value | Keep cost `UNKNOWN` |
| Budget owner exists | Named role and evidence of authority or actual spend | Participant assumes another team would pay | WTP remains unvalidated |
| Willingness to pay exists | Executed paid engagement or money received for the bounded outcome | Interest, survey answer or letter of intent | No commercial proceed |

## Neutral interview guide

Ask about the most recent qualifying transaction: What did the buyer request?
What existed at that moment? What was missing? Who owned each response? What took
longest? What was challenged or rejected? What changed the decision date? Who had
authority? What could not leave the supplier? What workaround was used? What was
actually paid for? What result would have justified payment? After the factual
account is complete, ask what properties would make any evidence-assistance
output useful or unacceptable. Ask for contrary examples where the process
was quick, cheap or satisfied by existing tools.

Do not demo, pitch, state benefits, suggest regulatory compliance, disclose prices
or lead the participant toward the hypothesis.

## Checkpoints and stopping

Interview counts are management checkpoints, not statistical proof. Maintain a
coverage matrix for supplier role, buyer role, blocker, approver, transaction
outcome and contrary case. Review after every qualifying transaction. Stop for
thematic sufficiency only when the predefined categories have coverage and two
successive independently sourced transactions add no material category; report
the narrow sample and recruitment route. Stop immediately for unsafe disclosure,
coercion, adviser-identified legal blocker or owner withdrawal. Failure to obtain
safe access is a feasibility failure and leaves the customer hypothesis
unresolved; it does not falsify customer pain. Contrary buyer evidence that the
work is immaterial or adequately handled counts against the hypothesis without
an arbitrary account threshold.

Discovery can establish described workflows, observed costs and commitment
signals. It cannot produce `PAID_VALIDATED`; only an authorised completed paid
engagement can do that.

Recruitment is `OWNER_GATED` and `NOT AUTHORISED`. The template contains
placeholders only and is not outreach copy.
