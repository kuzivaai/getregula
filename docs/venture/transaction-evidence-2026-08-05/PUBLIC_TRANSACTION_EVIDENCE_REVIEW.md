## 2. Search protocol and deviations

`PROTOCOL.md` froze H1, H0, 14 questions, evidence classes and the stopping rule
before transaction-specific searching. Thirteen query rounds screened 231 returned
results. Results were separated into buyer, regulator, transaction, supplier,
industry, vendor and user classes. Rounds 11 through 13 added no new qualifying

The search interface did not retain second-level timestamps for rounds 1 to 6;
their date, order and exact queries remain recorded. Rounds 7 to 13 have separate

## 4. Included, contrary and null evidence

Direct evidence supports three propositions. Admiral's live page is current
buyer evidence, but its exact version at the cut-off is unresolved and therefore
corroborative rather than independently controlling:

- Admiral publicly describes due diligence for suppliers providing AI,
  including risk management, data protection and accountability requirements.
- Direct Line and Lloyd's describe structured supplier onboarding, risk-based
  due diligence and ongoing assurance. Lloyd's identifies data protection,
  information security and continuity questionnaires.
- Named UK insurance cases show AI used in claims routing, fraud referral or
  broader claims operations.

Those propositions do not combine into H1. The search found no public artefact
showing a small UK AI supplier answering a UK general insurer's production-
onboarding request for claims-triage evidence. It found no named supplier account
of repeated evidence rework, material delay, rejection or cost in that exact
transaction. It found no H1 budget owner or source-code request.

No direct source contradicted the possibility of H1. The decisive result is
insufficient exact evidence, not proof of market absence.

## 9. Regula-observable evidence

The current Regula boundary is static, local analysis of repositories and related
artefacts. In principle it can point to some source-linked implementation signals,
dependencies, configuration and documentation. The public buyer evidence instead
spans organisational controls, contracts, data protection, security assurance,
continuity, incidents, operational monitoring, model performance, human oversight
and buyer judgement.

No defensible proportion can be calculated without a real permissioned buyer
questionnaire and evidence set. The materiality of source-code evidence to H1 is
therefore `UNVERIFIED`. Passing repository tests does not answer this question,
and current technical fit remains failed until a new independent evaluation.

## 10. Non-technical evidence

The following cannot be established reliably by source scanning alone:

- corporate responsibility and approval records;
- contractual allocations, warranties and insurance;
- data provenance, lawful processing decisions and DPIAs;
- operational performance, fairness and monitoring results;
- incident response in practice;
- business continuity and financial standing;
- independent assurance and certificates;
- buyer acceptance and legal classification.

These are not edge cases. They are central to the public insurer requirement
classes found.

## 15. Strongest case against the decision

Private insurer procurement material is rarely public. Admiral's explicit AI-
supplier due diligence, Direct Line's rigorous onboarding, Lloyd's recurring
questionnaires and multiple named claims-AI deployments make it plausible that
an exact transaction exists behind confidentiality boundaries. A small number of
carefully controlled conversations could discover evidence unavailable online.

That case supports the proposition that public research has limits. It does not
justify paid professional review now: there is still no demonstrated H1 supplier
burden, buyer acceptance criterion or Regula-observable material component.

## 16. Falsifier

Reconsider abandonment if a permissioned, recent transaction supplies all of:

1. a small UK AI supplier and UK general-insurer buyer;
2. production onboarding for AI-assisted claims triage;
3. the actual evidence request and accepting roles;
4. recorded rework, delay, rejection or material cost;
5. a material technically observable evidence subset; and
6. a credible route to compare Regula against the existing manual workflow.

Interest, a generic questionnaire or a claims-AI demo alone is insufficient.

## 17. Owner actions and prohibited actions

`REASONED`: no owner spend is justified for Regula-specific professional review
