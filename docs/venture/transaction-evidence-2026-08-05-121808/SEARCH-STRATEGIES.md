# Preregistered search strategies

STATUS: PREREGISTERED BEFORE SUCCESSOR SEARCH

Search families are reviewed separately: (A) official regulators and insurance
bodies; (B) direct insurer onboarding and transaction artefacts; (C) named
supplier-insurer transactions; (D) attributable supplier burden; (E) substitutes;
and (F) technical observability. Exact queries, UTC times and returned/screened/
included counts will be recorded in `SEARCH-LOG.csv`.

PRISMA-S informs reporting completeness, not a claim that this is a systematic
review. PRESS informs query review, but no independent information specialist
review occurred. Garousi, Felderer and Mäntylä inform separation and appraisal
of grey and academic evidence. RFC 9309 controls crawler behaviour; its rules are
not access authorisation. Official public procurement APIs are analogue sources
only and cannot prove private-insurer demand.

After each round, unresolved atomic claims are ranked qualitatively by decision
impact, public settleability, retrieval cost and access risk. No fabricated
probabilities or generic market-rescue queries are permitted.
