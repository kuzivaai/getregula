## Hypotheses

H1: A small UK AI supplier is responding to a UK general insurer's
production-onboarding evidence request for an AI-assisted claims-triage system.

H0: The exact transaction is unsupported, inaccessible, adequately served by
existing alternatives, or contains too little technically observable evidence
to justify further Regula-specific investment.

No H1 component is assumed. H1 will not be broadened. At most one adjacent
transaction may be assessed, and only if direct evidence makes it materially
stronger.

20. What result requires abandonment?

## Method and gate

The review is multivocal: official, buyer, transaction, named-supplier,
peer-reviewed, industry, vendor and user sources remain separate. Prior searches
are inherited evidence; at least three materially different successor rounds
will target the highest-impact gaps. Three consecutive rounds adding no new
decision category establish searched-source saturation only.

`SUPPORTED_FOR_HUMAN_DISCOVERY` requires direct buyer workflow, independent
supplier burden, overlapping unrelated requirements, plausible participant
access, a material technically observable component and no decisive substitute
redundancy. Public research cannot validate willingness to pay.
