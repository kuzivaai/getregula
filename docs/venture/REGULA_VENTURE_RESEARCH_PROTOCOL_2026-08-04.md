## Purpose

Determine whether Regula has a genuinely innovative, commercially viable and
scalable venture path. A negative outcome, service-only outcome, substantial
pivot, partner-first outcome, open-source-only outcome or STOP is acceptable.
Research cannot overturn the existing commercial STOP gate. Only new evidence
that directly addresses its failed gates can do that.

This unit does not change product code, detectors, scanner rules, benchmarks,
thresholds, public claims, pricing, versions, founder records, legal documents,
release configuration or deployment state. It does not contact users, spend
money or submit anything externally.

## Evidence classes

- `REPOSITORY_MEASURED`: retained command output against a named commit/tree.
- `REPRODUCED`: independently rerun with the same normalised result.
- `PRIMARY_SOURCE_VERIFIED`: legislation, regulator, standards body,
  specification, official register or official release record.
- `PEER_REVIEWED_SUPPORTED`: study supports a method, not Regula's outcome.
- `VENDOR_CLAIMED`: vendor statement not independently reproduced.
- `USER_REPORTED`: identifiable review, issue or discussion.
- `MULTI_SOURCE_SIGNAL`: repeated independently across credible sources.
- `HUMAN_VALIDATED`: properly recorded human research.
- `PAID_VALIDATED`: paid engagement or signed commitment.
- `REASONED`: inference with assumptions and falsifier.
- `UNVERIFIED`: unsettled.
- `CONTRADICTED`: directly refuted.

Vendor claims will not become product facts. Complaints will not become demand.
Competitor presence, GitHub stars, regulation, research interest and market-size
reports will not become willingness to pay or Regula revenue.

## Method

### Work package 1: reconstruct truth

Recompute Git and generated facts. Read the complete commercial evidence and
raw results. Recalculate fractions and intervals. Classify prior findings as
`CURRENT`, `VERSION_BOUND`, `STALE`, `CONTRADICTED` or `UNVERIFIED`.

### Work package 2: external research

Use cut-off-bounded primary sources for law, standards, protocols, procurement
requirements and official product releases. Use vendor sources only for vendor
claims. Use reviews and forums only for user-experience signals. Seek contrary
evidence. Competitor expansion stops only after two successive structured
passes add no materially new category, buyer job or business model.

Every material source records publication date, event/effective date where
relevant, retrieval date, visible last-update date, version and cut-off status.
Post-cut-off live updates cannot establish the cut-off state without a dated or
versioned record.

### Work package 3: synthesis

Build a claim-level evidence matrix with supporting and contrary sources,
limitations, decision effect and falsifier. Score raw opportunity dimensions,
then test three decision profiles: `COMMERCIAL-FIRST`,
`DEFENSIBILITY-FIRST`, and `CAPITAL-EFFICIENT-VALIDATION`. Select exactly one
beachhead and one first paid offer, or STOP. Build bottom-up low/base/high
scenarios from explicit sourced assumptions, never a generic market report.

## Precommitted decision constraints

- Research cannot produce `PAID_VALIDATED`.
- A plausible architecture cannot demonstrate viability or scalability.
- A winning thesis must not depend on automated legal classification.
- A platform is deferred until repeated paid work demonstrates stable reusable
  structure.
- A software recommendation must beat or materially complement a transparent
  manual baseline on the named buyer job.
- Existing commercial STOP remains unless new technical, demand, operational
  and claim-integrity evidence directly closes the failed gates.
- Founder and immigration criteria cannot supply market evidence.

## Serious-candidate dimensions

Report, without hiding the decision in one score: pain severity, frequency,
urgency, budget ownership, willingness evidence, technical feasibility, time to
validation, delivery complexity, liability, regulatory risk, data access,
interoperability, scalability, gross-margin potential, defensibility,
replicability, distribution, founder fit, partner dependence and evidence
strength.

## Falsification and stopping rules

Reject or stop a thesis if its named customer will not provide representative
inputs, the transparent baseline is non-inferior, independent reviewers reject
the outputs, no paid commitment follows bounded validation, human delivery
effort does not decline, evidence cannot be reused lawfully, liability exceeds
the attainable margin, or the required data boundary is unacceptable.

All owner contact, payment, contracting, professional advice, ownership/IP
change, title change, publication, pricing change, product change, release and
immigration action remains owner-gated.
