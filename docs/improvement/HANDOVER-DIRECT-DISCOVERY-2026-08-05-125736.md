## Discovery and data method

The population requires personal participation within 12 months in an active
enterprise AI-software sale, onboarding or procurement review with a qualifying
diligence function. Hypothetical accounts, commentators, marketing-only vendors,
unsafe disclosure and Regula-selected enthusiasts are excluded.

Interviews start with an uninterrupted recent-event account, then neutral probes;
technical observability is analyst-coded afterwards. Stage A is 2 supplier-side
plus 2 buyer/reviewer-side participants across at least 2 organisations,
denominator 4. It is an access/method check, not market evidence. Later batches
are 2, with review no later than 12; this is a management ceiling, not saturation.

The schema excludes identities, direct contacts, employer/customer/product
names, contracts, repositories, code, security findings and special-category
data. Requirements link evidence state, roles, acceptance and observability.
Burden/delay retain ranges, units, basis, role and uncertainty. Inadvertent
confidential disclosure requires interruption, omission/redaction and the
reviewed incident/deletion procedure.

H2 is not created. Before any future H2 gate, corpus controls must enforce event
and account uniqueness, valid ranges, observed basis for incumbent inadequacy
and independent analyst review. Three selected transactions cannot establish
prevalence, recurrence, market or demand. Willingness to pay stays unvalidated.
