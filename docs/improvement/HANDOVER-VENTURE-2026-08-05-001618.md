## 10. Technology and standards findings

Standards can improve structure, portability, provenance or integrity when a
named recipient consumes them. They do not prove underlying evidence or create a
moat. A signature proves a key signed bytes; identity and time require additional
trusted binding. Structured assurance concepts and evidence freshness are
testable internal hypotheses. Agent standards are emerging. Advanced privacy
technology is deferred until a threat model and real disclosure refusal exist.

## 11. Theory findings and decision effects

Resource-based reasoning placed any prospective advantage in lawful decision
data and reviewer learning, not features. Platform economics rejected an exchange

## 16. Strongest case against it

Incumbent platforms, standard questionnaires, certifications, auditors and
consultants may already handle the work adequately. Code observes only a small
part of buyer evidence. Material reviews demand organisational, contractual,
security, financial and independent evidence; immaterial ones may need only a
short form. Local processing is replicable. Confidential evidence may not be
reusable. Expert review creates liability and linear labour. There is no payment,
buyer acceptance, comparative advantage or technical efficacy on real labels.

## 20. Kill and pivot criteria

Stop for absent repeated pain, no representative access, buyer rejection,
baseline non-inferiority, no paid validation, non-declining effort, unusable reuse,
unacceptable liability or recurring technical failure. Build software only after
comparable paid cases show recurrence, reuse, falling effort and preserved buyer
acceptance. A non-binding expression of interest is not commercial proceed
evidence.
