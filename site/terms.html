Site terms

Terms for using this website, stated plainly. Last updated 14 August 2026.

What this site is

getregula.com is the website of Regula, an open-source tool that scans code for patterns that may need AI governance review and records deployment facts that code cannot show. The site provides information, guides, a browser assessment, and links to the software. It is operated by the project's maintainer (see the About page).

Not legal advice

Nothing on this site is legal advice, a compliance determination, a certification, or a conformity assessment. Content and tool output indicate what may need review; whether a law applies to a real system, and what it requires, are questions for a qualified professional with knowledge of your facts. Where a page names a regulatory date or provision, we cite the source, and regulations can change after a page is published.

No paid services are offered on this site at present

The pricing page describes a planned consultant service. Describing it is not an offer for sale: no payment can be made and no booking can be placed through this site at present, and the page says so. If that changes, the service, its provider, its price, and its terms will be published before anything can be bought.
