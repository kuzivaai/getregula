Paid support is not yet available. No payment is currently processed, no booking is accepted, and no consultant has been assigned through this site. Disabled labels are intentional: they prevent a visitor mistaking a roadmap item for a purchasable service.

The core scanner processes files locally and does not upload them. A future consultant engagement will need a separately agreed evidence-sharing method, scope, privacy notice, retention rule, cancellation terms, and professional boundary before payment is enabled.

What stays free, and what paid support adds

The payment boundary is expert time and accountability, not access to basic safety information. Regula will continue to show what it observed, what remains unknown, and why it cannot make a determination.

Free product

  • Questions, code indicators, and unresolved facts
  • Transparent rule and evidence paths
  • Local generation of reviewer-completable scaffolds
  • Clear limitations and referral cues

Paid human work

  • Context gathering and evidence-quality review
  • Interpretation by a named consultant within a stated scope
  • Prioritisation, workshop time, and documented follow-up
  • Escalation to qualified legal or specialist advice where needed

Questions

What remains free?

The browser questions, local scanner, observed indicators, unresolved facts, and existing CLI commands remain free. Paid support adds human review and a scoped service; it does not hide the tool's evidence or limitations.

What would a consultant review provide?

The proposed service includes a scoped evidence review, one live walkthrough, a prioritised action brief, and a written list of facts or specialist decisions still required. The exact service, consultant, duration, price, and terms will be published before booking opens.

Can I pay or book now?

No. There is no live checkout or booking flow. Payment will not be enabled until fulfillment, availability, privacy, tax, cancellation, refund, and professional-boundary controls are ready and tested.

Will the consultant give legal advice?

Not unless the named consultant is separately qualified and engaged to do so. AI governance support can help organise facts, evidence, and actions, but legal conclusions must be referred to an appropriately qualified professional.
