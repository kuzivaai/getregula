Questions

Will the free tier ever become paid?

No. The CLI is the distribution channel. Every command that exists today stays free forever. Evidence packs are a paid deliverable on top of the free tool.

What do I actually get?

A ZIP bundle containing JSON scan results, gap analysis, Annex IV documentation, remediation plan, and a SHA-256 integrity manifest. Starter packs are Ed25519-signed. Professional packs add a conformity assessment pack, Declaration of Conformity template, and a CycloneDX SBOM.

Is this a subscription?

No. Each payment generates one evidence pack for one project at a point in time. Run it again next quarter if your codebase has changed.

Can my auditor verify the pack?

Yes. Each bundle includes a standalone verify.py script that checks SHA-256 hashes of every file against the manifest. Signed packs also include an Ed25519 signature your auditor can verify independently.
