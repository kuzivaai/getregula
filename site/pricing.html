Why test prices are visible. GBP 950 and GBP 650 per day are hypotheses to test through concrete scoped proposals; nobody has accepted or rejected either figure in the current evidence register. They are shown so a conversation can test a real boundary rather than a vague “would you pay?” question. A focused assessment for a founder is not equivalent to an enterprise readiness programme. The dated competitor-price check and its limits remain available in the repository, but competitor prices do not validate ours.

Payment gate not active. No payment or booking runs on this site today. Every paid action on this page is an email. There is no checkout, no card capture and no booking flow, and the named consultant and full terms will be published before anything can be bought. If your team needs a multi-system or organisation-wide review, the same email reaches us: support@getregula.com. The core scanner processes files locally and does not upload them; optional commands such as the governance news feed, vocabulary refresh and RFC 3161 timestamping do make outbound requests, and the timestamping command sends hashes rather than code.

What we are, stated plainly. There is no EU legal entity behind this offer and no professional indemnity cover is in place. Nothing on this site, and nothing produced by the tool or by a paid session, is legal advice. Regula indicates where a person should look; it does not determine your risk tier, your obligations or your compliance, and a qualified professional must settle anything that turns on a legal conclusion.

Questions

What remains free?

The browser questions, local scanner, observed indicators, unresolved facts, and existing CLI commands remain free. Paid support adds human review and a scoped service; it does not hide the tool's evidence or limitations.

What would a consultant review provide?

The service hypothesis includes a scoped evidence review, one live walkthrough, a prioritised action brief, and a written list of facts or specialist decisions still required. GBP 950 for the fixed scope and GBP 650 per day are unvalidated test prices. The seller, named consultant, duration, insurance, fulfilment controls and full terms must be resolved before any service or booking opens.

Can I pay or book now?

No. There is no live checkout or booking flow. Payment will not be enabled until fulfillment, availability, privacy, tax, cancellation, refund, and professional-boundary controls are ready and tested. You can email us at any time; a conversation costs nothing and commits you to nothing.

Will the consultant give legal advice?

Not unless the named consultant is separately qualified and engaged to do so. AI governance support can help organise facts, evidence, and actions, but legal conclusions must be referred to an appropriately qualified professional.
